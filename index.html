    The House

    Every Agha fragrance begins as a place: a smoke-lit room, an ice cave, a garden after rain. It ends as something you carry close.

    House of Agha composes eaux de parfum around a single world at a time, and dresses each one in its own bottle, colour and light.

    The story of the House
    1. 01 One worldEach fragrance is composed around a single place.
    2. 02 Its own lightA bottle, colour and mood made for that world alone.
    3. 03 Worn closeEaux de parfum meant to stay near the skin.
