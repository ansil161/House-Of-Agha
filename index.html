02 — THE HOUSE MANIFESTO

LESS MARKETING.
MORE CONCENTRATION.

AGHA was founded on a simple refusal: to compromise scent integrity for mass production. Where traditional fragrance houses dilute extracts to 12–15% alcohol solutions, we compound our extraits de parfum at an uncompromised 35% oil concentration.

Every formula is macerated for 90 days in temperature-controlled dark chambers, allowing natural botanicals to deepen into complex, atmospheric signatures that evolve over 14+ hours on skin.

05 — CRAFT & ORIGIN

THE ART OF PERFUMERY

  1. 01

    BOTANICAL EXTRACTION

    We harvest rare flowers and resins at peak potency—wild iris from Florence, pink pepper from Madagascar, and wild agarwood from Assam. Cold-pressed to retain fragile olfactory notes.

  2. 02

    90-DAY MACERATION

    Unpolished raw extraits age in dark obsidian vessels for 90 days. This controlled maturation allows top, heart, and base notes to fuse into a seamless sensory composition.

  3. 03

    FLACON ARTISANSHIP

    Each heavy-glass bottle is hand-polished, individually numbered, and capped with solid magnetic obsidian glass to protect the living elixir within from UV degradation.
