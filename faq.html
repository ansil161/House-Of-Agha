FORMULATION & CRAFT

CONCENTRATION & MACERATION

WHAT DOES 35% CONCENTRATION MEAN?

Most commercial Eau de Parfum formulas contain 12% to 18% fragrance oil mixed with alcohol. AGHA Extraits de Parfum contain 35% pure perfume oils, producing exceptional sillage and 14+ hours of continuous skin presence.

WHY ARE THE EXTRAITS MACERATED FOR 90 DAYS?

Raw natural botanical extracts (such as Florentine Iris and Cambodian Oud) require time to marry with carrier oils. Aging in dark obsidian vaults for 90 days softens alcohol topnotes and develops deep resinous warmth.

ARE AGHA FRAGRANCES UNISEX?

Yes. We compound extraits based on raw mood and presence rather than gender marketing. Each scent reacts uniquely to individual skin chemistry.

ORDERS & CLIENT CARE

SHIPPING & CONCIERGE

DO YOU OFFER COMPLIMENTARY SAMPLES WITH FULL BOTTLES?

Yes. Every full 50ml flacon includes a complimentary 5ml matching trial atomizer. You may test the 5ml atomizer first before unsealing the main bottle box.

CAN I HAVE INITIALS ENGRAVED ON THE BOTTLE CAP?

Yes. Members of the AGHA Private Vault receive complimentary laser engraving of up to 3 initials on the solid magnetic obsidian cap.

WHAT IS YOUR RETURN POLICY?

If the 5ml trial atomizer does not suit your skin, return the unsealed 50ml main flacon box within 30 days for a full refund or exchange.
