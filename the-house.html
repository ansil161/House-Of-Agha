HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

THE HOUSE OF AGHA

Founded on an uncompromising refusal: to dilute beauty for speed. We create extraits de parfum compounded at 35% oil concentration, macerated in temperature-controlled dark chambers.

01
PROVENANCE

RAW BOTANICAL EXTRACTION

Every formula starts with sustainable botanical harvesting: wild agarwood from Assam, Florentine iris roots, and Madagascar pink pepper harvested at twilight.

02
MATURATION

90-DAY DARK MACERATION

Unlike mass commercial scents, raw AGHA extraits age untouched for 90 days in obsidian vessels, allowing top, heart, and base notes to meld into a seamless sensory composition.

03
ARTISANSHIP

FLACON CRAFTING & SEALING

Each heavy-glass bottle is hand-polished, individually numbered, and capped with magnetic obsidian glass to protect the living elixir within from UV degradation.
