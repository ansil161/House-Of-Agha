About the House

Seven fragrances.Seven places.

House of Agha makes eaux de parfum. Each one is built around a single place, and dressed in a bottle of its own.

Meet the fragrances

We start with a place: a smoke-lit room, an ice cave, a palace garden. Then we build the scent, the bottle and the photograph around it, so each fragrance feels like somewhere you have been.

One house, seven worlds.
