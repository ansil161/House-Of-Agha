About House of Agha

Seven fragrances.Seven places.

House of Agha makes eaux de parfum. Each one is built around a single place, and dressed in a bottle of its own.

Meet the fragrances

7fragrances in four scent families

  • Woody & Smoky
  • Fresh & Aquatic
  • Floral & Luminous
  • Aromatic & Green

One world per bottle

A smoke-lit room, an ice cave, a palace garden. Every fragrance is built around its own place, then photographed in it.

How a fragrance comes together.

Place

A place comes first

Black sand, cold stone, a garden in late sun. Each fragrance starts from one setting and stays true to it.

Materials

Materials from that world

Tobacco leaf and moss, bergamot and jasmine, oud wood and sea salt. The notes follow the place.

Bottle

A bottle of its own

No shared flacon. Each fragrance gets its own glass, cap and colour, so you can tell them apart on the shelf.
