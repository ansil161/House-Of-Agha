Shipping & returns

Domestic shipping

All orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Delivery then takes 2–5 days to metro cities and 3–7 days to other areas. We use express shipping at no extra cost, and shipping is free on prepaid orders.

Some cities may take longer than 5 days because of public holidays, weather or transport. If your order still shows unfulfilled after 5 days, or no tracking email arrives, write to info@aghaperfumes.com.

International shipping

International shipping is charged separately and shown at checkout. Prices include taxes but exclude customs duties, which are borne by the customer. Your local customs office can tell you the rates for parcels from India.

Returns

We accept returns only if the product is damaged, defective or incorrect on delivery. It must be unused, in its original packaging, and reported within 48 hours of delivery. Used perfumes, opened packages and damage from misuse cannot be returned.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the item we will let you know. Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

How to request a return

Email info@aghaperfumes.com with your order ID, the reason for the return, and clear photos or video of the item received. We respond within 24–48 hours.
