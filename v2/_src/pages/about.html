The name Agha

آقا

A title of respect, authority and nobility in Qutb Shahi culture.

In Persian
Agha means lord, master or gentleman. It was used to address people of high status, such as noblemen and respected elders.
In Ottoman times
It named senior officials and military leaders, such as the Janissary Agha who commanded the elite troops.
Across the region
In many Middle Eastern and South Asian societies it is a term of respect for elders, teachers and community leaders.
Today
It is still a respectful address in Iran, Turkey, Afghanistan and parts of India, often joined to a first or last name.
