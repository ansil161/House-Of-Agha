01 — LOGISTICS & GUARANTEES

SHIPPING &
RETURNS POLICY.

Every AGHA Extraits de Parfum order is packaged in climate-balanced obsidian casing and dispatched via express tracked courier worldwide. Experience our formulations risk-free with our complimentary sampler guarantee.

GLOBAL FULFILLMENT

EXPRESS DELIVERY

COMPLIMENTARY DOMESTIC EXPRESS

We offer complimentary express shipping on all domestic orders over $150. Orders placed before 14:00 CET are dispatched same-day with 1–2 business day delivery guarantee. Signature is required upon delivery.

DUTIES & TAXES INCLUDED

International delivery is operated via DHL Express across 80+ countries. All import duties, taxes, and customs clearance charges are pre-calculated at checkout with zero additional fees upon delivery.

TEMPERATURE-CONTROLLED TRANSIT

To safeguard our delicate 35% oil concentration against thermal degradation during transport, every package features thermal insulating liners and obsidian shock-absorbent sleeves.

CLIENT SATISFACTION

SAMPLE & RETURN RISK-FREE

THE COMPLIMENTARY SAMPLER PROMISE

Every full bottle order includes a matching 2ml sample vial. We invite you to test the 2ml vial on your skin before unsealing the full bottle. If the scent does not resonate with your skin chemistry, return the unopened full bottle for a full refund.

30-DAY HASSLE-FREE RETURNS

Unopened, sealed items in original obsidian packaging may be returned within 30 days of receipt. Contact our Concierge at concierge@aghaperfumes.com to receive a pre-paid courier shipping label.

REFUND PROCESSING

Once received and inspected at our Paris atelier warehouse, refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

PERSONAL ASSISTANCE

HAVE A SPECIFIC TRANSIT QUESTION?

Our Concierge Team is available 7 days a week to track active shipments, arrange courier holds, or process bespoke return requests.
