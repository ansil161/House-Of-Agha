# House of Agha — homepage imagery

Source: the House of Agha Google Drive asset folder (campaign photography per fragrance).
Downloaded on 23 Sep 2026 at 2400 px through Drive's resizer (visually identical to the
5.6K originals) and converted to WebP. Each asset ships as `<name>.webp` (2000 px wide for
landscapes, 1200 px for portraits and squares) and `<name>-sm.webp` (1000 / 700 px).
Files sit flat in `assets/` because Shopify does not allow sub-folders there.

Product cutouts (`hoa-product-*`) were trimmed to the bottle, their studio background lifted
to pure white, and each bottle placed at the same height on a 1200×1500 canvas so the
collection grid lines up. On the page they blend onto warm paper with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.

Not used: the "concepts drafts" folder (AI mock-ups) and the older artboards other than the
studio cutouts.
